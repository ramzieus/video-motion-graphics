# Level 1 Arabic Reel Example (1080x1920)

A vertical motion graphic composition adhering strictly to the **Level 1 Prompt Specification**.

## Key Specifications
- **Canvas Resolution**: `1080x1920` (9:16 Vertical)
- **Frame Rate**: `30 FPS`
- **Total Duration**: `10.0 seconds` (300 frames)
- **Pacing**: Scene transitions every `2.5 seconds` (75 frames).
- **Aesthetic**:
  - Calm and confident dark background (`#0A0D14`).
  - Two colors (`#0A0D14` obsidian slate & `#FFFFFF` pure white).
  - Single-word highlight in vibrant amber gold (`#F59E0B`).
  - Large, clear Arabic typography with RTL alignment.
- **Safe Zone**:
  - `Top: 180px`
  - `Bottom: 320px`
  - `Sides: 90px`
- **Ending**: `[Name]` + "تابعني للمزيد" ("Follow me for more").
