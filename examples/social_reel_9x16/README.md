# Social Reel & Short Example (9:16 Vertical)

This example demonstrates a high-impact, fast-tempo 9:16 vertical motion graphic designed for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

## Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: `9:16` (1080x1920)
- **Frame Rate**: `30 FPS`
- **Total Duration**: `10.0 seconds` (300 frames)
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Vivid Neon Pop (`#050505` black with high-contrast pink/violet glow)

## Vertical Safe Margins
- **Top 15% (0–288px)**: Kept clear of text to avoid overlapping the platform username, audio attribution, or search bar.
- **Bottom 20% (1536–1920px)**: Kept clear of critical CTA text to prevent occlusion by captions, like/comment buttons, and description tags.
- **Center Focus (288–1536px)**: High-density focal area holding the kinetic typography and phone mockup.
