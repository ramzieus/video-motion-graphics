# Curated Visual Aesthetics & Color Palettes for Motion Graphics

When generating motion graphics, cohesive art direction is essential. Use these pre-calibrated aesthetic kits for instant, professional styling.

---

## 1. Modern Dark SaaS (Highest Converting / Recommended Default)
Best for: B2B products, AI applications, developer tools, cloud platforms.

- **Background**: `#090D16` (Deep Obsidian Blue)
- **Card Background**: `#131B2E` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border
- **Primary Accent**: `#6366F1` (Indigo 500)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#8B5CF6` (Violet 500)
- **Highlight Glow**: `#38BDF8` (Sky Blue)
- **Text Primary**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
- **Text Muted**: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
- **Font Pairing**: `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Signature Visuals**: Glassmorphism backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), radial gradient glow orbs behind cards, 1px subtle borders.

```css
/* Ambient glow effect */
background: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 30%, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25) 0%, transparent 70%);
```

---

## 2. Minimalist Clean (Apple / Linear Aesthetic)
Best for: Consumer products, design agencies, luxury hardware, productivity apps.

- **Background**: `#F9FAFB` (Ultra Light Gray) or pure `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Card**: `#FFFFFF` with `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)` border & soft shadow
- **Primary Accent**: `#111827` (Deep Jet Black)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#3B82F6` (Electric Blue)
- **Text Primary**: `#111827` (Gray 900)
- **Text Muted**: `#6B7280` (Gray 500)
- **Font Pairing**: `Geist Sans`, `SF Pro Display`, or `Inter` (Font weight 600+)
- **Signature Visuals**: Razor-thin 1px borders, generous whitespace, butter-smooth spring curves (`mass: 1, damping: 20`), monochrome contrast.

---

## 3. Cyberpunk & Dev Terminal
Best for: CLI tools, cybersecurity, blockchain, backend infrastructure, open-source repos.

- **Background**: `#0D1117` (GitHub Dark) or `#050505` (Pitch Black)
- **Terminal Chrome**: `#161B22` with `#30363D` border
- **Terminal Header**: 3 macOS dots (`#FF5F56`, `#FFBD2E`, `#27C93F`)
- **Primary Neon**: `#00F5D4` (Electric Cyan)
- **Accent Neon**: `#10B981` (Matrix Emerald)
- **Warning/Error**: `#F43F5E` (Rose Neon)
- **Font Pairing**: `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code`
- **Signature Visuals**: Blinking cursor (`_`), typing text interpolator, grid lines background, scanline overlays.

---

## 4. Fintech & High Finance
Best for: Payments, banking, analytics, investment platforms.

- **Background**: `#040D1A` (Deep Oceanic Navy)
- **Card Background**: `#0A192F`
- **Accent Positive**: `#10B981` (Vibrant Emerald)
- **Accent Neutral**: `#0EA5E9` (Cyan 500)
- **Accent Gold**: `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber for tier badges)
- **Font Pairing**: `Outfit` or `Manrope`
- **Signature Visuals**: Glowing sparklines, animated counter tickers with currency symbols, curved gradient area charts.

---

## 5. Vivid Pop & Social Energy
Best for: 9:16 TikTok/Shorts hooks, creator tools, youth-oriented apps, gaming.

- **Background**: `#0F051D` (Vivid Midnight Violet)
- **Gradient 1**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #EC4899 0%, #8B5CF6 50%, #3B82F6 100%)`
- **Accent Yellow**: `#FACC15` (Attention Pop)
- **Accent Pink**: `#F43F5E` (Hot Coral)
- **Font Pairing**: `Clash Display` or `Poppins` (Extra Bold / Black 900)
- **Signature Visuals**: Big kinetic typography, word-by-word bouncing stamps, sticker badges, dynamic camera shakes on bass drops.
